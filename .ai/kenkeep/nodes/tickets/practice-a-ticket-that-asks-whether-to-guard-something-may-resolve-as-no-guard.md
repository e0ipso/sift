A ticket phrased as "decide whether to guard X" is answered by a decision, not by a guard. When the answer is *no guard*, that is a complete resolution: record the reasoning — the measurements taken and why the check would not hold — in the ticket's `resolution` and in prose beside the rule it would have guarded, and archive it.

Do not file a follow-up ticket to hold the reasoning. A follow-up implies the work is outstanding, so the next agent re-opens a question that was already answered, and the answer decays into a backlog row instead of living where someone about to add the check will read it.
